Field Notes: Fundamentals of Guiding Models

A foundation model does not know what you want until you frame the prediction problem. When you enter a prompt, you are setting the initial conditions of a massive probabilistic system.

The Core Anatomy of an Operational Prompt

  • Instruction: An unambiguous verb-driven directive specifying the exact operation.
  • Context: Domain boundaries, target persona, and background circumstances.
  • Source Data: The raw, unformatted material to analyze, summarize, or extract.
  • Output Contract: Hard boundaries on format, length, and schema (e.g. JSON, bullet list).

Field Notes: Mechanics of Next-Token Prediction

Models break words into numerical representations called tokens. A prompt acts as an attention weight modifier, narrowing down billions of candidate tokens into precise contextual completions.

Inference Parameters

  • Temperature: Flattens or sharpens the softmax curve over the vocabulary.
  • Top-P (Nucleus Sampling): Discards low-probability tails dynamically.
  • Context Window: The fixed memory horizon across prompt and generated tokens.
