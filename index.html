Introduction to prompt engineering.

A language model produces a continuation of the information it receives. Prompt engineering is the practice of making that continuation useful, relevant, and reliable.

Field Notes: 01 · Fundamentals

Big Idea: A language model produces a continuation of the information it receives. Prompt engineering is the practice of making that continuation useful, relevant, and reliable.

The Central Question

Imagine giving a language model the words: “The most important discovery of the experiment was…”. Many continuations are possible. The model could finish a scientific report, invent a dramatic story, or produce a short summary. The words surrounding the request determine which kind of continuation is likely.

1. How Did We Get Here?

Era Key development What changed?
1940s onward Statistical language models and Markov approaches Predict text from a limited preceding context.
2014 Sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) models Translate or transform one sequence into another.
2015 Attention in neural sequence models Allow a decoder to access relevant portions of the input.
2017 The Transformer architecture Use attention as the central mechanism for representing relationships.
2018+ GPT: Generative Pre-trained Transformer Pretrain a generator, then adapt it to useful tasks via prompting.

3. The Three Limitations

  • A language model is not automatically a search engine: It can produce plausible text without having retrieved or verified a source.
  • Context is finite: A model processes only the content available to it within its supported context limit.
  • Fluent does not mean correct: A well-written explanation can still contain factual or logical errors.

Task Typical heuristic Trade-off

Stage What happens? Example
